Immerse yourself in the captivating contrast of ancient traditions and cutting-edge modernity on this 10-day journey through Japan's two most iconic cities. Begin in the electric energy of Tokyo — explore Shibuya, Shinjuku, Harajuku, and the serene Meiji Shrine. Then glide on the Shinkansen to Kyoto, where bamboo forests, geisha districts, and hundreds of temples await.

This package includes return flights from Johannesburg, accommodation in carefully selected 4-star hotels, daily breakfast, guided city tours, a day trip to Nara to see the famous deer park, and a traditional tea ceremony experience.

Day 1-2 · Arrival in Tokyo

Fly overnight from Johannesburg to Tokyo Narita. Hotel check-in, rest and an introductory evening walk through the Shinjuku district.

Day 3 · Tokyo City Tour

Guided full-day tour: Asakusa & Senso-ji Temple, teamLab Planets digital art museum, Akihabara electronics district, Shibuya Crossing at dusk.

Day 4 · Day Trip to Nikko

UNESCO World Heritage site. Elaborate shrines, waterfalls, and mountain scenery just 2 hours north of Tokyo.

Day 5-6 · Hakone & Mt Fuji views

Overnight in Hakone ryokan. Sunrise views of Mt Fuji, open-air museum, ropeway and crater lake boat ride.

Day 7 · Shinkansen to Kyoto

High-speed bullet train to Kyoto. Afternoon at leisure in Gion district — catch a glimpse of a maiko.

Day 8-9 · Kyoto & Nara

Fushimi Inari torii gates, Arashiyama bamboo grove, traditional tea ceremony, and a half-day trip to Nara's deer park and Tōdai-ji temple.

Day 10 · Departure

Transfer to Kansai International Airport for the return flight to Johannesburg.
